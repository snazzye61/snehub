ABOUT SNE VAULT

Making account access simpler.

A marketplace built to make purchasing social media accounts easier for everyone.

SNE VAULT is a digital marketplace created to give people a simpler way to discover, purchase, and manage social media accounts from one organized platform.

We focus on a straightforward customer experience: clear account categories, simple purchasing, accessible order information, and support when a buyer needs help logging in or managing a purchase.

Built by Elias Etinge

Elias Etinge is the CEO and software engineer behind SNE VAULT. He created the website with a practical goal: to make purchasing social media accounts easier, more organized, and more accessible for everyone.

Through technology and continuous improvement, Elias is building a platform where customers can browse with confidence, understand what they are purchasing, and receive support after an order.
