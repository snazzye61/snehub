SNE VAULT HELP CENTER

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers about buying, payments, downloads, and account access.

What is SNE VAULT?

SNE VAULT is a digital marketplace designed to make browsing, purchasing, and managing social media accounts simple and organized.

How do I purchase an account?

Browse the available categories, review an account listing, and follow the purchase steps. You can use your wallet balance or an available payment method to complete the order.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Wallet deposits support Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money, Visa, Mastercard, and USDT. For USDT payments, select the correct TRC20 or ERC20 network before sending funds.

How do I add funds to my wallet?

Open Wallet from your dashboard, enter the amount, select your payment method, and continue to payment. Your balance is updated after the payment is confirmed.

Where can I find an account I purchased?

Open Downloads from your dashboard. Your available purchases appear there with their order information and access guide.

How do I log in to an account I purchased?

Open the access tutorial in your dashboard and follow the steps for the platform you purchased. Keep the delivered login details private and use a trusted device.

What should I do if my login details do not work?

Contact support through WhatsApp, phone, or the Contact page. Include your order number and the email connected to your SNE VAULT account. Never send your password or security codes publicly.

How do I contact support?

You can contact us through WhatsApp at +237 652 717 609, call +237 652 717 609 or +1 202 888 3254, or use the contact options on our Contact page.

Can I get a refund or replacement?

Refund and replacement eligibility depends on the order and the issue reported. Contact support promptly with your order number so the team can review the situation and explain the available next steps.

Is my information secure?

We use reasonable safeguards to protect account and order information. For more details about data collection, storage, and your rights, read our Privacy Policy.

Still need help?

Our support team can help with an order, payment, or account-access problem.
